# Erratum to the 2026-08-02 regulatory-coverage result

The coverage percentages reported on 2026-08-02 were computed from samples
whose sentences were accidentally reversed within each source paragraph before
deterministic every-*k*-th selection. The implementation therefore violated the
protocol requirement that candidates remain in document order.

The causal defect, unchanged correction boundary, reproduction commands, and
artifact hashes are recorded in `CORRECTION-PROTOCOL-2026-08-11.md`.

## Consequence

The original samples, labels, and report remain committed as historical
evidence, but they are **not a valid current estimate of coverage** under the
stated sampling protocol. This applies to:

- the 5% strict and 12% ambient-deontic normative coverage figures;
- the 25% D1 and 3% D2 strict coverage figures;
- all blocker frequencies, ceiling analyses, and comparisons derived from
  those labels;
- the scored prediction outcomes that cite those figures.

The corrected run changed 38 of 60 normative sample texts and 20 of 50
definitional sample texts. Because the corrected samples are not labeled, no
replacement percentages are reported. Qualitative observations from the old
run may be hypotheses, but they are not corrected findings.
